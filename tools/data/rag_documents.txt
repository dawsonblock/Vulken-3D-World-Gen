Buildings in the desert often use sandstone and have thick walls to keep cool. Roofs are typically flat; courtyards and shaded walkways are common. Palm wood and adobe are frequent materials. Water collection systems via cisterns are practical.
Elven architecture features graceful curves, slender towers, and integration with nature. Structures often weave around ancient trees; materials include white stone, living wood, and crystal. Light bridges and leaf-patterned roofs are hallmark elements.
